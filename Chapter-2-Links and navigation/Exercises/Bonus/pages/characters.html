Characters

The people of Panem

Katniss Everdeen

The protagonist and female tribute from District 12. A skilled hunter and archer who volunteers to take her sister's place in the Hunger Games.

Peeta Mellark

The male tribute from District 12. A baker's son who has secretly loved Katniss for years. Uses his charm and strength to survive the Games.

Haymitch Abernathy

The only living Hunger Games winner from District 12. Serves as mentor to Katniss and Peeta, though initially appears as a drunk.

Gale Hawthorne

Katniss's best friend and hunting partner. He cares deeply for Katniss and her family, representing the life she leaves behind.

Effie Trinket

The Capitol escort for District 12's tributes. Initially superficial but grows to care for Katniss and Peeta.

President Snow

The tyrannical leader of Panem who sees Katniss as a threat to his power after her defiance in the Games.
