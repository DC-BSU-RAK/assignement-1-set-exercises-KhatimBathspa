Critical Reception

Roger Ebert - Chicago Sun-Times

★★★★

Peter Travers - Rolling Stone

★★★★

"A thrilling, thought-provoking adventure that captures the spirit of Suzanne Collins' novel. The film's social commentary is as sharp as Katniss' arrows."

Rotten Tomatoes

84% Fresh
