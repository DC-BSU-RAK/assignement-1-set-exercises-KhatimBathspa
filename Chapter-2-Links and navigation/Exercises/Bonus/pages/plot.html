Plot Summary

The story of Katniss Everdeen

The Hunger Games Story

Twelve districts and the Capitol make up the authoritarian nation of Panem in the dystopian future depicted in The Hunger Games. A lottery is used to choose two teenage representatives from each district each year to compete in The Hunger Games, a televised event in which competitors (also known as "tributes") must battle to the death in a perilous outdoor arena until only one is left.

Katniss Everdeen, 16, offers to step in for Primrose, her younger sister, who has been chosen as District 12's female tribute. Under the supervision of their inebriated mentor, Haymitch Abernathy, a former Hunger Games champion from District 12, she and her male counterpart, Peeta Mellark, are sent to the Capitol to train and get ready for the Games.

Katniss depends on her keen intuition and survival abilities during the Games. Katniss and Peeta develop a romance as the Games go on in order to win over sponsors and ensure their survival. The Gamemakers declare that two tributes from the same district can win when there are just a handful left. After discovering Peeta, who has suffered severe injuries, Katniss tends to his medical needs.

The Gamemakers revert their earlier rule modification, allowing only one winner, when they are the final two tributes left. In defiance, Katniss and Peeta plan to kill themselves by eating deadly berries simultaneously, which would mean that there would be no winner at the Games. They are both proclaimed winners at the last second.

Despite their survival and return home, Katniss finds out that she has become a symbol of hope for the oppressed people of Panem and that her acts in the arena have sparked rebellion in the districts.
