Production Details

The Hunger Games was filmed primarily in North Carolina, with locations including the Dupont State Forest, the Henry River Mill Village, and the Crowders Mountain State Park. The production designer was Philip Messina, who created the stark contrast between the impoverished District 12 and the opulent Capitol.

The film's score was composed by James Newton Howard, while the costume design for the Capitol citizens was created by Judianna Makovsky, featuring bold colors and futuristic styles to emphasize the Capitol's extravagance compared to the districts.
