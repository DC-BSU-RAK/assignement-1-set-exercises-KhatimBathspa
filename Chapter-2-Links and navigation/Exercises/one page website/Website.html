The Hunger Games

"May the odds be ever in your favor."

Plot Overview

Twelve districts and the Capitol make up the totalitarian nation of Panem in this dystopian future. Two teenage representatives from each district are chosen by lottery each year to compete in the televised struggle to the death known as The Hunger Games. A 16-year-old girl from District 12 named Katniss Everdeen offers to compete in the Games in place of her sister. Her bravery makes her a symbol of resistance to the repressive government.

Main Characters

  • Katniss Everdeen - The brave protagonist
  • Peeta Mellark - Fellow tribute and ally of Katniss
  • Gale Hawthorne - Katniss's childhood friend
  • President Snow - Ruthless leader of Panem
  • Effie Trinket - The Capitol escort for District 12 tributes

Cast & Creative Team
